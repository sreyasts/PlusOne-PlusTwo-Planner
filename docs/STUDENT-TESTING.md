# Try a real study session

Use [Mission PlusTwo](https://mission-plustwo.web.app/) as a guest. Participation is voluntary. Do not send names, school details, marks, account information or backup files in public reports.

1. On your usual phone or computer, choose your stream, target date and realistic daily study hours. Record anything unclear during setup.
2. Find today's assigned subject and topic. Open its resources and decide whether the suggested portion is realistic for your available time.
3. Study one portion, mark it done, reload and check that progress remains. Try Undo if you marked the wrong item.
4. After one online visit, turn off the network and reopen the app. Check today's plan and completion controls. External study links and cloud sync need a connection.
5. Reopen on the next day. Today's timetable should advance with the calendar, earlier unfinished work should appear under Pending, and completed work should remain recorded.

Report the device/browser, what you tried, expected result, actual result and whether the problem repeats. Use the **Study session feedback** GitHub issue template; redact personal information from screenshots. A keyboard or Malayalam-language attempt is useful when those match your normal workflow.

If you do not use GitHub, describe the problem to the person who shared the app. They can record the issue with your permission, without identifying you.

For maintainers: record observed problems and fixes with permission. Count participants only when actual independent participation is verified. Do not count automated tests, your own sessions, stars or repository visitors as students. A task checkmark alone does not prove learning. Prioritize repeated blockers over feature requests that add complexity.
