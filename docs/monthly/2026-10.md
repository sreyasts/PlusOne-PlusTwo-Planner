## 1. Product Engineering Highlights

### Major Architectural & Pedagogical Additions
- **Socratic AI Study Companion (`src/utils/ai_tutor.js`)**:
  - Engineered an open-source pedagogical prompt adapter connecting Kerala SCERT/DHSE curriculum data directly to LLMs (OpenAI ChatGPT & Google Gemini).
  - Implemented 4-stage active retrieval: diagnostic questions, student response checkpoints, 5-point rubric feedback, and board past-year exam questions (PYQs).

## 3. OpenAI for Open Source Program Alignment

Mission PlusTwo actively bridges curriculum open data with AI models to assist high school students in underserved regional educational ecosystems:
- **Pedagogical LLM Grounding**: Rather than generating static summaries, the open-source engine crafts Socratic prompts that guide LLMs to test students on specific Kerala DHSE chapter concepts and evaluate their answers using official scoring rubrics.
- **Accessibility & Digital Equity**: Completely free, ad-free, and privacy-preserving (0 tracking of PII, GDPR & DPDP compliant).
- **Transparent Open Source Governance**: Full MIT licensing, standardized JSON schemas for curriculum pacing, and extensive regression test suites.
