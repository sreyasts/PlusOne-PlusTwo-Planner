# HSS Planner mission

Updated 18 October 2026.

## Project purpose

Maintain a useful, free study planner for Kerala DHSE students. Prioritize reliable daily planning, clear progress tracking and recovery after missed study days.

## Product mission

Help Kerala Plus Two and Plus One improvement students choose a realistic daily study load, complete the next task, and recover after missed days. Keep guest planning usable without an account and preserve existing plans when improving the interface.

Prioritize a clear setup, today's tasks, completion tracking, schedule recovery, offline reliability, and reproducible tests. Add features when they solve a demonstrated student problem.

## Evidence standard

- Repository age, commits, tests and releases demonstrate engineering history; they do not establish independent adoption.
- The owner reports that the five existing stars came from their account and a friend's accounts. Do not call them organic adoption. The independence or actual use of the fork is unverified.
- Independent active users, repeat study sessions, external contributions and school adoption are currently unverified. Do not turn local browser counters or repository visitors into student counts.
- Do not claim statutory compliance or educational outcomes without evidence. Describe implemented behavior and its limits.

## Current priorities

- Today's calendar timetable, overdue work and tomorrow's preview remain separate.
- Preserve existing progress when restoring backups or updating the app.
- Check mobile, keyboard and offline workflows before shipping changes.
- Collect voluntary feedback about actual study tasks; distinguish intended audience from verified usage. See [student testing](STUDENT-TESTING.md).
